SafeScan AI Shield - install (Chrome / Edge, desktop)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode.
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder.
4. Reload open tabs. The shield appears bottom-right on websites.
5. Click the shield or toolbar icon for Links, SMS, Mail, QR (camera, upload, snip) and Scan selected text.
